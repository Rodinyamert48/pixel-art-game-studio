# Code of Conduct

[Back to README](../README.md)

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in Pixel Art Game Studio (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions) can
expect a welcoming, harassment-free experience, regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, sexual identity, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about work, not about people.
- Credit others' work. Do not submit art, text, or code you do not have the right to share, and never
  submit material copied from commercial games.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, sexualized content, threats, doxxing or
publishing others' private information, sustained disruption, and encouraging any of these.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban participants who
violate this code. Report problems to the maintainers at `<maintainer-contact>`.

> **Maintainer note:** `<maintainer-contact>` is a placeholder. Whoever publishes this repository
> should replace it with a real, monitored contact address (or a link to a private reporting form)
> before accepting outside contributions.

Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will not retaliate against people who report in good
faith.
